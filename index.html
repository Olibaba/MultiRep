About MultiRep

A distribution partner built on trust, continuity, and retail access.

Since 2001, MultiRep has supplied imported consumer goods to Costa Rican retail channels. Our model is simple: source quality products, maintain dependable supply, and support retailers with categories that move on shelf.

What we bring to the retail channel

  • Imported products across candy, snacks, food packaging, household goods, and apparel.
  • Commercial relationships with supermarket and grocery buyers in Costa Rica.
  • Local distribution support for international brands entering the market.
  • Practical category execution through shelf checks, replenishment support, and sales follow-up.
